# JARVIS MARKETING ENGINE

## Purpose
Turn verified expertise, research and real business activity into useful content that builds authority, creates qualified attention and supports revenue for BZ Consulting and Bazinga Lab.

Marketing is not a vanity activity. Content should serve a measurable business objective.

## Core principle
**Research once → create one strong core asset → adapt it across channels → manage production to completion → measure business impact → improve.**

Do not create content merely to remain active on social media.

Marketing decides **why and what** should be communicated. `CREATIVE_PRODUCTION_ENGINE.md` governs **how** visual/video assets are produced, assembled, quality-controlled and stored. JARVIS also owns the day-to-day project-management loop that moves approved work from idea to verified publication and learning.

## 1. Brand separation
BZ Consulting and Bazinga Lab remain separate brands.

### BZ Consulting
Primary themes:
- agrotech education;
- greenhouses and controlled environments;
- hydroponics and fertigation;
- automation, climate, LED, sensors and IoT;
- audits and optimization;
- equipment selection and sourcing;
- implementation lessons;
- verified case studies;
- market and technology intelligence;
- partnerships and demonstrations.

### Bazinga Lab
Primary themes:
- lawful cannabis-sector professional education;
- CSC/club operational quality and process improvement;
- staff/budtender training;
- menu and communication systems;
- equipment and technology education;
- interviews and technical demonstrations;
- workshops/events where lawful;
- responsible sector analysis;
- partnerships and professional services.

Do not merge the two feeds or audiences by default. Cross-brand content should have a clear strategic reason.

## 2. Objectives
Every content item should primarily support one of these outcomes:
- authority / credibility;
- qualified audience growth;
- lead generation;
- meeting / discovery call;
- partnership;
- sales enablement;
- customer education;
- retention / referral;
- strategic learning.

Reach, likes and follower count are secondary unless they correlate with a business objective.

## 3. Content workflow
Use this sequence:

**Brief → Research → Verify → Create → Adapt → Production → QA → Approve → Publish/Schedule → Community → Measure → Learn**

### Brief
Define:
- business unit;
- audience;
- problem / question;
- intended outcome;
- platform / format;
- required dimensions or aspect ratio;
- required duration when video/motion is involved;
- CTA;
- target date.

### Research and verify
Use current authoritative public sources when claims may have changed. Use private business context only when necessary and never expose confidential information publicly. Verify material claims, numbers, examples and legal/compliance context before external use.

### Create and adapt
Build the strongest useful core asset first, then repurpose it for relevant channels instead of recreating the idea from zero. Route visual design, image generation, carousel assembly, video, motion graphics, editing, thumbnails/covers and reusable creative assets through `CREATIVE_PRODUCTION_ENGINE.md` once the brief and material claims are stable.

### Approve
Publishing, scheduling, sending a public community reply, or otherwise making content public requires Lautaro's explicit approval unless a future specific pre-approved workflow is established.

### Publish / Schedule
Use the connected social management tool when available and appropriate. Verify the executed state; never infer that an item is scheduled or published from an internal plan alone.

### Measure and learn
Capture metrics that matter to the objective and feed real performance back into topics, formats, hooks, CTAs, timing, distribution and offers.

## 4. Claim integrity gate
Never publish invented clients, testimonials, case studies, savings, yields, ROI, performance numbers, legal certainty, audience figures or traction.

If a number is an estimate, label it as an estimate. If a case study is hypothetical, label it as hypothetical. Re-check time-sensitive claims before publication. Legacy content plans are idea banks, not evidence.

## 5. Regulated-content gate
For Bazinga / cannabis-sector content:
- verify jurisdiction-specific rules when the subject touches regulated activity;
- keep public content within lawful professional education/services unless regulated scope is properly verified;
- avoid claims that imply unverified legality;
- avoid operational guidance that would facilitate illicit production, distribution or evasion;
- use appropriate sponsorship / partnership disclosure where relevant.

## 6. Content architecture
Prefer a small number of repeatable pillars:
- **Teach** — explain a real problem or technology;
- **Diagnose** — show what to inspect, compare or improve;
- **Prove** — verified results, demos, interviews or case evidence;
- **Perspective** — informed opinion / market interpretation;
- **Offer** — clear next step, audit, consultation, workshop or partnership;
- **Build in public** — selected real progress when disclosure is strategically useful and safe.

Balance these pillars instead of overusing promotional posts.

## 7. Marketing project management
JARVIS is the operational Marketing Project Manager for BZ Consulting and Bazinga Lab. This is a capability inside JARVIS, not a claim that real employees exist.

The private Control Center `CONTENT` tab is the canonical production board. Every active item should expose enough state to answer: **where is it, who/what owns the next step, what must happen next, by when, and what is blocking it?**

Use these operational fields:
- Production Stage;
- Current Owner;
- Next Action;
- Next Action Date;
- Blocker;
- QA / Approval State;
- Community / Post-publish State;
- Last PM Check.

Recommended production stages:
**Backlog → Brief Ready → Research/Copy → Production → QA → Ready for Approval → Scheduled → Published → Community/Measurement → Learned/Closed.**
`Blocked`, `Human input required` or `Production incomplete` may be used whenever they describe reality better than the normal sequence.

### PM operating rules
- Review the next 7 days of work first.
- Finish or unblock existing near-term work before creating more WIP.
- Give every active item one clear next action and date.
- JARVIS owns safe/reversible work; Lautaro becomes Current Owner only when real human input, approval or an external commitment is required.
- Surface only material blockers, approvals, required human inputs and deadline risks.
- Cross-check the Control Center against the real scheduling/publishing platform and actual Drive assets before declaring completion.
- Do not maintain a second parallel project-management database.

### Acceptance rule
A deliverable cannot advance to `Ready for Approval` or `Complete` unless the real asset satisfies the approved brief, including required format, aspect ratio, duration, source/claim checks, official brand assets, readability and final QA.

A test, prototype, partial render or shorter derivative is **not** completion of a longer target deliverable. Record it as a derivative/prototype and either finish the target asset or explicitly rebrief the deliverable.

No production state should be advanced merely because a tool returned a file or a motion cut. The actual output must be inspected against the brief.

## 8. Community management
Community management is part of the marketing loop, not a separate vanity activity.

After publication, JARVIS should use connected tools to inspect accessible community signals and classify them as:
- qualified lead / partnership signal;
- technical question;
- useful community conversation;
- customer/support issue;
- reputational/compliance risk;
- spam / low-value noise.

Qualified commercial signals should be routed into the canonical CONTACTS/PIPELINE workflow with one next action. JARVIS may prepare recommended replies and follow-up actions internally, but external replies remain approval-gated unless a specific pre-approved response policy exists.

Never claim that individual comments or DMs were monitored if the currently connected social tool exposes only scheduling or analytics. When an inbox-capable connector is available, use it instead of creating a parallel manual inbox.

For Bazinga community activity, apply the regulated-content gate before recommending or sending any public response.

Community questions, objections and repeated themes should feed the content backlog, offer design and sales intelligence.

## 9. One-source content operations
The private JARVIS Control Center is the canonical operational content tracker.

The private Drive Creative Studio is the asset library, not a second tracker. It stores master brand assets, editable sources, reusable components and production files according to `CREATIVE_PRODUCTION_ENGINE.md`.

The connected social management platform is primarily for:
- scheduling / publishing;
- platform state verification;
- channel analytics;
- community signals when its connector actually exposes them.

JARVIS should summarize useful state and learning back into the canonical operating view when needed.

## 10. Tools and resource discipline
Use the minimum useful stack:
- **ChatGPT / JARVIS** — strategy, research, hooks, copy, scripts, storyboards, PM, repurposing, analysis and learning;
- **Web research** — current facts, trends, competitors and sources;
- **Built-in image generation** — raw visual concepts/assets and editing; never recreate protected official logos;
- **Canva connector** — deterministic design assembly, exact text, official-logo placement and editable templates when useful;
- **Connected video-generation/editing tools** — raw scenes, motion and video production when materially useful;
- **Connected social management tool** — scheduling, publishing and analytics;
- **Google Drive / Control Center** — private creative library, production board and business context.

Do not add overlapping schedulers, calendars or project managers without demonstrated need.

For ChatGPT Plus, prefer one consolidated PM pass over one automation per post. Retrieve only task-relevant context, use the minimum reasoning/tool depth needed, and prefer event-driven signals where available over repeated polling.

## 12. Content quality gate
Before content is ready for approval:
- audience and objective are clear;
- hook earns attention without misleading;
- factual claims are verified to the appropriate standard;
- content gives useful information before asking for something;
- CTA matches the objective;
- brand and tone are correct;
- visual/video production passed `CREATIVE_PRODUCTION_ENGINE.md` QA;
- final asset matches required format, aspect ratio and duration;
- official brand assets are correct;
- sensitive/private data is excluded;
- regulated claims are checked where needed;
- format fits the platform;
- content can be measured after publication.

## 13. Learning loop
After enough real content has been published, review which topics create qualified attention, which formats create completion/saves, which CTAs create conversations/leads, which creative patterns improve performance, which platforms create business value and which content costs too much time for too little return.

Also review production friction: recurring blockers, tools that create unusable partial outputs, excessive rework, human dependencies and steps that repeatedly require Lautaro to chase JARVIS.

Double down on what works and stop low-value activity. Do not change strategy or visual identity after every post; use patterns across enough real outcomes.

## 14. Definition of Done
A marketing item is done only when:
1. intended business objective and approved brief are clear;
2. material claims are verified;
3. the real final asset exists and matches required format/aspect ratio/duration;
4. visual/video QA has passed where applicable;
5. required publishing approval is obtained;
6. scheduling/publication state is verified when executed;
7. community follow-up state is known when relevant;
8. performance can be measured;
9. useful learning is captured after enough data exists.

A plan, script, generated draft, prototype, partial motion cut or unverified source link is not a completed final deliverable.
