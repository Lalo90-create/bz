# Bazinga Lab — Offer Architecture

Bazinga Lab sells professional services to CSC/club and adjacent cannabis-sector operators, keeping the commercial offer simple, useful and compliant.

## Offer 1 — Club Audit
Entry service to understand the club's current operation.

Possible outputs:
- member/customer journey review;
- menu and communication review;
- staff workflow review;
- operational pain points;
- training gaps;
- technology/CRM gaps;
- priority action plan.

## Offer 2 — Budtender Training
Possible modules:
- product knowledge;
- customer communication;
- responsible-service principles;
- menu presentation;
- sales/service consistency;
- internal SOPs.

## Offer 3 — Menu & Communication System
Possible outputs:
- menu architecture;
- category logic;
- product information templates;
- visual/content briefing;
- update process;
- staff cheat sheets.

## Offer 4 — Events & Workshops
Possible services:
- educational events;
- tastings/workshops where lawful;
- partner activations;
- staff sessions;
- community events;
- event format and supplier coordination.

## Offer 5 — Quality & Process Review
Possible services:
- service standards;
- process consistency;
- quality documentation;
- internal checklists;
- improvement plan.

## Offer 6 — CRM & Technology Support
Possible services:
- CRM needs assessment;
- member communication workflow;
- data/process organization;
- technology/vendor research;
- implementation support.

## Offer 7 — Partnership / Sourcing Support
For lawful products and professional services only:
- vendor research;
- introductions;
- referral partnerships;
- technology/service sourcing;
- B2B coordination.

## Commercial ladder
Whenever possible use:

**Small paid entry → Core service → Recurring support → Referral**

Examples:
- Audit → Training → Monthly process support
- Menu review → Menu system → Monthly updates
- CRM audit → Vendor selection → Implementation support
- Event pilot → Event calendar / recurring collaboration

## Rule
Never propose or execute regulated or plant-touching activity without verifying legality and scope first.
