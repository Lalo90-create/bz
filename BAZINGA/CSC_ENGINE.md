# Bazinga Lab — CSC / Club Commercial Engine

## Objective
Turn relationships with CSC, clubs and other lawful cannabis-sector operators into professional, repeatable revenue.

## Global scope
The engine is global, but the operating model must adapt by jurisdiction.

Default expansion ladder:
**Mazarrón / Cartagena / Murcia / La Manga → Spain → Europe → international markets.**

Outside Spain, JARVIS must first identify:
- what the local operator model is;
- what terminology is appropriate;
- which services are lawful;
- what restrictions affect promotion, events, data, products or operations.

## Priority order
1. Warm contacts / existing relationships
2. Strong local opportunities with fast access
3. Spanish operators with clear commercial fit
4. European operators / partners with scalable potential
5. Global opportunities with strong score, access or strategic value
6. Partners who can introduce multiple operators or markets

## Qualification
For each operator, JARVIS should determine:
- Is the organization active and reachable?
- Do we know someone inside or have a warm path?
- What operational problem is visible or likely?
- Which Bazinga offer fits?
- What is the smallest paid first step?
- Can the service become recurring?
- Can a successful case produce referrals?
- Is the offer compliant in that jurisdiction?

## Opportunity score
Use the central `JARVIS/MONEY_ENGINE.md`.

Add these Bazinga-specific signals qualitatively:
- warm relationship;
- clear decision-maker;
- visible pain point;
- fast-to-deliver service;
- recurring potential;
- referral potential;
- market expansion value.

Do not create a second numeric scoring system here.

## Outreach sequence
Do not mass-spam.

Preferred sequence:
1. Research the operator and market
2. Identify one specific reason to contact
3. Choose ONE entry offer
4. Prepare a short personalized message
5. Lautaro approves before sending
6. Follow up
7. Move the lead through CRM
8. Record what worked

## Entry-offer logic
Prefer easy-to-understand offers first:
- mini audit;
- menu / communication review;
- staff training session;
- event/workshop pilot where lawful;
- CRM/process review.

Avoid leading with a huge consulting package before trust exists.

## Recurring conversion
After a successful entry service, evaluate:
- monthly menu / communication support;
- recurring staff training;
- quarterly operational review;
- event calendar;
- CRM/process support;
- partner/vendor coordination.

## Weekly commercial benchmark
As an initial benchmark:
- 10 qualified operator leads;
- 5 personalized outreach drafts;
- 3 follow-ups;
- 1–2 concrete conversations;
- one tested offer;
- one learning captured into the playbook.

These are starting benchmarks, not guarantees. Replace them with real conversion data as soon as possible.
