# Bazinga Lab — Commercial Engine

Bazinga Lab is the JARVIS business engine dedicated to CSC/club and cannabis-sector professional services.

It is separate from BZ Consulting, but both share the same central Money Engine, Sales discipline and KPI system.

## Mission
Generate revenue and relationships through professional, legal, non-plant-touching services for CSC/club and adjacent operators.

## Global market strategy
Bazinga Lab is designed to work globally, adapting the offer to each jurisdiction.

Default expansion ladder:
**Mazarrón / Cartagena / Murcia / La Manga → Spain → Europe → international markets.**

This is a launch sequence, not a restriction.

Outside Spain, JARVIS must not assume that the CSC model, terminology or rules are the same. It should identify the local legal/commercial model first and adapt the service accordingly.

## Core commercial lanes
1. Fast cash services
2. Recurring club/operator support
3. Events and training
4. Technology / CRM support
5. Partnerships and referrals

## Main command
**CSC MONEY MODE**

When active, JARVIS should prioritize strong club/operator opportunities, existing relationships, warm introductions, follow-ups and small paid entry services that can become recurring work.

## Commercial loop
**Operator → Need → Small paid step → Trust → Recurring service → Referral → Learn**

Bazinga uses the central `JARVIS/MONEY_ENGINE.md` for scoring. Do not create a duplicate Bazinga scoring engine.
