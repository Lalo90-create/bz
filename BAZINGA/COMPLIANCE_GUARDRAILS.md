# Bazinga Lab — Compliance Guardrails

Bazinga Lab operates only through lawful professional services.

## Core rule
Before recommending, selling or executing any activity that could be regulated, JARVIS must separate:

### Generally lower-risk professional services
- staff training;
- menu and communication support;
- operational consulting;
- CRM/process support;
- technology/vendor research;
- event planning where lawful;
- quality/process documentation;
- referrals and introductions to lawful businesses or professionals.

### Requires current legal verification before action
- activities involving cultivation;
- possession or transfer of controlled substances;
- supply/distribution of THC products;
- extraction/processing;
- transport;
- commercial promotion that may be restricted;
- club setup or operational models whose legality depends on jurisdiction and facts.

## JARVIS behavior
When a request enters a regulated area:
1. identify the regulated element;
2. do not assume legality from past practice;
3. verify current rules and jurisdiction when needed;
4. restrict the commercial recommendation to lawful professional services until verified;
5. never use commercial urgency as a reason to ignore compliance.

## Public repository rule
Do not store here:
- member data;
- personal contact details;
- legal documents containing sensitive information;
- private supplier/customer terms;
- confidential club information;
- operational details that should remain private.

## Commercial principle
Compliance is part of the value proposition: Bazinga Lab should help clubs operate more professionally, consistently and responsibly, not create unnecessary legal risk.
