# BZ — Company Profile

## Purpose
BZ is a business platform focused on practical consulting, commercial development and technology solutions around cultivation, agrotech, hydroponics, controlled environments and related services.

## Main positioning
BZ should not present itself only as a seller of equipment. The preferred positioning is:

**consulting + solution design + sourcing + implementation support + training + commercial network.**

## Core areas
- Hydroponics and controlled-environment cultivation
- Greenhouses and indoor cultivation infrastructure
- Automation, climate control, sensors and IoT
- LED and cultivation technology
- Consulting and project design
- Installation/implementation coordination
- Training and operational support
- Commercial development and supplier sourcing

## Geographic priority
Current priority: Spain, especially the Murcia / Cartagena / Mazarrón area, with ability to work on broader Spanish and international opportunities.

## Business principle
BZ should prioritize offers that are:
1. understandable to the client;
2. commercially valuable;
3. modular and easy to quote;
4. capable of creating recurring or follow-on work;
5. supported by reliable suppliers and partners.

## JARVIS instruction
When working on BZ, always think in terms of:

**problem → client → offer → value → proof → next action → revenue.**

Do not reduce BZ to generic marketing. Focus on concrete commercial opportunities.
