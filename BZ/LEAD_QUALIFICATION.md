# BZ — Lead Qualification

Use this before spending serious time on a prospect.

## Minimum information
- Company / organization
- Location
- Sector
- What they appear to need
- Decision-maker or likely contact
- Why BZ may be relevant
- Source of the lead
- Next action

## Score each lead 1–5
- Problem fit
- Budget likelihood
- Urgency
- Access to decision-maker
- BZ delivery fit
- Revenue potential

## Interpretation
- 25–30: work now
- 19–24: qualified
- 13–18: nurture / test
- 6–12: low priority

## JARVIS output for each qualified lead
1. Why this lead matters
2. Best BZ offer
3. Main pain point hypothesis
4. Best contact angle
5. Suggested first message
6. Suggested follow-up
7. Commercial priority

## Rule
Do not invent private facts about the lead. Separate verified information from hypotheses.
