# BZ — Proposal Template

Use this structure for a simple commercial proposal.

## 1. Client situation
What is happening now? What problem, limitation or opportunity has been identified?

## 2. Objective
What result should the project create?

## 3. Proposed solution
Describe only the components needed for this client.

Possible blocks:
- assessment / audit;
- design;
- equipment / sourcing;
- implementation support;
- training;
- optimization.

## 4. Deliverables
List exactly what the client receives.

## 5. Client responsibilities
What information, access, approvals or local resources must the client provide?

## 6. Timeline
Use realistic phases rather than vague promises.

## 7. Commercial terms
Insert final pricing, payment terms and validity only in the private client proposal, not in this public template.

## 8. Assumptions / exclusions
State what is not included and what depends on third parties, logistics, regulation or final site verification.

## 9. Next step
End with one simple action:
- approve discovery call;
- provide project data;
- approve site visit;
- approve proposal;
- schedule kickoff.

## JARVIS rule
A proposal must connect the price to a business result or risk reduction. Avoid dumping a product list without explaining value.
